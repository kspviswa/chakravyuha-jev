What do these numbers mean?
  • Speed — totalMs is the whole-run wall time; msPerStep = totalMs ÷ max(steps, 1). Lower is better.
  • Optimality — optimalSteps / steps, clamped to [0, 1]. 1.0 is perfect. A run that did not reach the centre scores 0; a board with no route at all scores null and is excluded from the mean rather than counted as 0.
  • Green — greenSteps / steps: how many of the run's moves were the model's own, played as given. The rest were red — corrected by the walk, because the model was unsure or its answer could not be played. The colour of the path is the run's real output; the walk can always overrule, so arriving is no longer in doubt.
  • Confident — confidentSteps / steps: how many of the run's moves were taken with TypeSafe reporting a clear read. A step Jev was sure about is a different measurement from a step it guessed at, even when both were right. Blank on runs recorded before it was captured.
  • Accuracy — stepAccuracy from the run record. reached is tracked separately, because “valid but never arrived” and “arrived” are different failures.
  • Variance / stddev — sample variance (n − 1 denominator). Variance needs at least two runs; below that it shows —, never a false 0.
  • Groups are difficulty · mode. Different kinds of run are never averaged together.
